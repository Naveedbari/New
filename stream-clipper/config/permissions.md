# Creators who allow clipping

Only streams from channels listed in the table below can be clipped. `clip.py` refuses everything else.

- **Channel URL**: the creator's channel page, e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/@SomeStreamer` or `https://www.twitch.tv/somestreamer`.
- **Permission**: where the written permission is kept (an email date, a screenshot file, a link to their clipping policy).

If a creator withdraws permission, delete their row and take their clips down in YouTube Studio.
